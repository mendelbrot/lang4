# g04.01
## nominative case

volitional agent, actor, or comparee. marks the deliberate initiator of an action or the subject being evaluated in a comparison.

- **agent**: triggered by transitive (`at`) verbs. marks the volitional doer acting upon an accusative (`om`) object.
- **actor**: triggered by intransitive process (`uc`) verbs. marks a volitional performer of a self-contained action (e.g. walking, running). contrasts with accusative (`om`) for non-volitional processes and experiencer (`et`) for involuntary sensations.
- **comparee**: triggered by comparative (`ak`) verbs. marks the entity being measured against the accusative (`om`) standard.

# g04.02
## accusative case

patient, theme, non-volitional intransitive subject, or standard of comparison. serves as the primary undergoer macro-role, with the verb determining the exact sub-meaning.

- **patient**: triggered by transitive (`at`) action or change verbs. marks an entity physically impacted, altered, created, or destroyed.
- **theme.transferred**: triggered by transfer, exchange, or communication verbs (e.g. give, send, buy, sell, trade). marks the item moved, conveyed, or targeted in the exchange, while the receiver takes recipient (`os`).
- **theme.stimulus**: triggered by sensory, emotional, or cognitive verbs paired with an experiencer (`et`). marks the object perceived, felt toward, or thought of.
- **subject.stative**: triggered by intransitive state (`id`) verbs. marks the entity holding a quality, identity, or static condition.
- **subject.process**: triggered by intransitive process (`uc`) verbs when the subject lacks volition. marks an entity passively undergoing a process or motion (e.g. a river flowing, ice melting, a person falling).
- **standard**: triggered by comparative (`ak`) verbs. marks the benchmark ('than' object) against which the nominative (`es`) comparee is measured.
- **clause.object**: triggered when attached to a discourse pointer (`this-after` or `that-before`) with a speech, perception, or cognition verb. embeds the referenced clause as the direct object.

# g04.03
## recipient case

dative receiver of transfer or communication.

- **transfer**: triggered by physical transfer or transaction verbs (e.g. give, hand, send, sell). marks the animate or institutional party receiving the accusative (`om`) theme.
- **communication**: triggered by speech, signaling, or demonstration verbs (e.g. tell, ask, show). marks the addressee receiving the message or information.

contrasts with allative (`an`), which marks spatial endpoints or inanimate destinations rather than a receiving party.

# g04.04
## beneficiary case

marks a participant who benefits from the clause.

- **benefit**: triggered with any action or state verb. marks the entity for whose advantage, well-being, or sake the event occurs ('for the benefit of').
- **proxy**: triggered with volitional action verbs when an agent acts on someone else's behalf. contrasts with substitutive (`og`), which emphasizes replacing someone rather than helping them.

# g04.05
## topic case

aboutness or subject matter ('about, regarding, concerning').

- **discourse**: triggered by communication, media, or documentation verbs and nouns (e.g. talk, read, write, story). marks the subject matter being discussed or depicted.
- **cognition**: triggered by mental verbs (e.g. think, wonder, worry, dream). marks the subject occupying the mind, especially when an accusative (`om`) object is already present or no direct object is taken.

never used for physical containment (use locative `in` or a compound) or transferred objects (use accusative `om`).

# g04.06
## experiencer case

conscious perceiver, feeler, or mental and sensory state holder.

- **perception**: triggered by sensory verbs (e.g. see, hear, smell, feel) with an accusative (`om`) stimulus. marks involuntary or receptive perception, contrasting with nominative (`es`) for deliberate observation (e.g. looking at, listening to).
- **emotion**: triggered by emotional or cognitive verbs (e.g. fear, love, enjoy, remember). marks the conscious feeler or thinker.
- **sensation**: triggered by intransitive state (`id`) or process (`uc`) verbs describing internal bodily or mental conditions (e.g. cold, tired, hungry). contrasts with accusative (`om`) to distinguish felt experience ('i am cold') from objective physical state ('the water is cold').

# g04.07
## locative case

static location in space or time ('at, in, on, during').

- **spatial**: triggered by place, surface, or container nouns. marks static physical position without motion toward or away.
- **temporal**: triggered by time nouns (e.g. day, night, noon, winter). marks the point or window in time when the event occurs.
- **discourse.concurrent**: triggered when attached to a discourse pointer (`that-before`). links two clauses as simultaneous ('meanwhile, at that same time').

# g04.08
## extent case

spatial extent or temporal duration ('over, across, for the span of').

- **spatial**: triggered by distance, length, or area nouns. marks the physical span covered by an action or state (e.g. walking five kilometers).
- **temporal**: triggered by duration nouns (e.g. hours, years, a moment). marks how long an action or state continues.

contrasts with locative (`in`), which locates an event at a point or frame rather than measuring its span.

# g04.09
## ablative case

starting point in space, time, or state ('from, origin, source, since').

- **spatial.origin**: triggered by motion or transfer verbs with place nouns. marks the point of departure or physical source.
- **temporal.start**: triggered by time nouns. marks the starting boundary in time ('since, starting from').
- **state.origin**: triggered by change or transformation verbs. marks the initial state or material from which something transforms.
- **discourse.sequence**: triggered when attached to a discourse pointer (`that-before`). links two clauses sequentially ('after that, and then, since then').

# g04.10
## allative case

endpoint in space, time, state, or exchange ('to, toward, destination, until, into').

- **spatial.destination**: triggered by motion or placement verbs with place nouns. marks the physical target or arrival point.
- **spatial.direction**: triggered by orientation or aimed verbs (e.g. face, shout, swing). marks direction without implying physical arrival or impact.
- **temporal.end**: triggered by time nouns. marks the ending boundary in time ('until, up to').
- **state.result**: triggered by change or transformation verbs (e.g. melt, freeze, turn). marks the resulting state or form ('into').
- **exchange.gain**: triggered by outgoing exchange verbs (e.g. sell, give, convert) where the accusative (`om`) is the item parted with. marks the money, item, or value gained in return ('sold for five coins').
- **discourse.endpoint**: triggered when attached to a discourse pointer (`that-before`). means 'until then, up to that point'.

# g04.11
## perlative case

path, route, or conduit traversed ('along, through, via').

- **spatial.path**: triggered by motion verbs with path, opening, or medium nouns (e.g. road, forest, tunnel, air). marks the route or medium moved through.
- **channel**: triggered by communication or transfer verbs with conduit nouns (e.g. mail, radio, messenger). marks the channel through which something is sent.

# g04.12
## manner case

how an action is performed, including similative resemblance ('-ly, in the way of, like, resembling').

- **quality**: triggered by quality, state, or abstract action nouns. modifies how the verb is carried out (e.g. quickly, gently, carefully).
- **similative**: triggered by concrete entity, creature, or role nouns (e.g. deer, child, doctor). expresses analogy or likeness ('like a deer, in the manner of a doctor').

contrasts with essive (`oc`), which marks actual held status rather than mere resemblance.

# g04.13
## instrumental case

reusable instrument, tool, or means ('using, by means of, with').

- **tool**: triggered by action verbs with concrete object nouns. marks a physical implement or device that is used without being consumed by the action.
- **means**: triggered by action verbs with abstract method, skill, or language nouns. marks a non-depletable technique or medium.

contrasts with consumptive (`af`), which marks depletable fuel, ingredients, or resources used up during the action.

# g04.14
## comitative case

companion or co-participant ('accompanying, together with').

- **companion**: triggered by animate nouns. marks someone participating alongside another argument in the clause.
- **accompaniment**: triggered by inanimate nouns. marks an item carried, worn, or present alongside without serving as a tool (e.g. arriving with a bag, tea with milk).

# g04.15
## abessive case

absence, lack, or exclusion ('without, lacking, except for, excluding').

- **absence**: triggered with any verb and a missing participant, tool, or feature ('without a map, lacking salt').
- **exclusory**: triggered in clauses containing universal or group quantifiers (e.g. all, everyone, every day). marks the member excluded from the set ('except for sam, excluding sunday').

# g04.16
## causal case

realized cause or reason ('because of, caused by, therefore'). reason quartet 1/4.

- **cause**: triggered by event, force, or state nouns. marks an actual, realized fact that brought about the clause.
- **reciprocity**: triggered by social or legal response verbs (e.g. thank, reward, fine, punish). marks the prior deed that prompted the response ('thanked her for her help').
- **discourse.consequence**: triggered when attached to a discourse pointer (`that-before`). links clauses by cause and effect ('because of that, therefore, so').

# g04.17
## purposive case

intended future motive or goal ('for the goal of, in order to'). reason quartet 2/4.

- **goal**: triggered by volitional action verbs with target nouns. marks a desired, unrealized outcome motivating the action.
- **discourse.purpose**: triggered when attached to a discourse pointer (`that-before` or `this-after`). links clauses by intention ('for that purpose, in order to').

contrasts with causal (`ot`), which marks a realized cause rather than an unrealized future goal.

# g04.18
## conditional case

hypothetical or uncertain condition ('given, in the event of, if'). reason quartet 3/4.

- **condition**: triggered by event or state nouns. marks a hypothetical circumstance under which the clause holds ('in case of rain, given enough time').
- **discourse.condition**: triggered when attached to a discourse pointer (`that-before` or `this-after`). links clauses conditionally ('if that is the case, then, on condition that').

# g04.19
## concessive case

obstacle overcome or contrast ('despite, in spite of, although, however'). reason quartet 4/4.

- **obstacle**: triggered by event, force, or state nouns. marks a real or hypothetical barrier that fails to block the clause ('despite the rain, in spite of fear').
- **discourse.contrast**: triggered when attached to a discourse pointer (`that-before`). links clauses by contrast ('however, but, despite that, nevertheless').

# g04.20
## vocative case

direct addressee.

- **address**: triggered by animate nouns, names, or titles. directly calls out or identifies the listener. stands outside the verb's argument structure.

# g04.21
## affective case

speaker's emotional attitude toward the clause.

- **attitude**: triggered by emotion or feeling nouns. expresses the speaker's personal emotional reaction to the entire event ('sadly, thankfully, to my surprise'), independent of any participant's feelings inside the clause.

# g04.22
## assessive case

speaker's intellectual evaluation or judgment of the clause.

- **evaluation**: triggered by value, truth, or judgment nouns. expresses the speaker's appraisal of the entire event ('fortunately, strangely, rightly, obviously').

# g04.23
## essive case

role, status, or capacity held during the clause ('as, in the capacity of').

- **capacity**: triggered by profession, role, or function nouns. marks an actual status or office held while performing the action ('works as a doctor, spoke as a leader').
- **stage**: triggered by life-stage or temporary identity nouns. marks the phase or status during which the clause holds ('as a child, while a student').

contrasts with manner (`us`), which only expresses resemblance rather than actual status.

# g04.24
## perspective case

frame of reference, viewpoint, or lens ('according to, from the viewpoint of, as for').

- **viewpoint**: triggered by animate or experiencer nouns. marks the person whose subjective judgment, standard, or perception frames the clause ('to me, according to sam').
- **domain**: triggered by abstract category or dimension nouns (e.g. cost, law, ethics, weather). narrows the scope or criterion being evaluated ('in terms of cost, as for weather').

# g04.25
## substitutive case

replaced entity or event ('instead of, rather than, in place of').

- **replacement**: triggered by any noun parallel to another participant in the clause. marks the entity bypassed or stood in for ('drank tea instead of coffee, went in place of alex').
- **discourse.alternative**: triggered when attached to a discourse pointer (`that-before`). links clauses as alternatives ('instead of that, rather than doing that').

# g04.26
## consumptive case

depletable resource used up, spent, or traded away ('consuming, expending, on, in exchange for'). strictly one-way; always marks what is surrendered or depleted.

- **fuel**: triggered by machine, process, or biological verbs. marks power or fuel burned to run the action ('runs on gas, runs on three hours of sleep').
- **material**: triggered by crafting, cooking, or building verbs. marks raw ingredients or single-use supplies consumed into the result ('baked bread using flour, painted using two cans').
- **expenditure**: triggered by effortful verbs with time, energy, or life nouns. marks finite personal or collective resources spent ('wrote it spending two hours, won at the cost of ten lives').
- **trade.away**: triggered by incoming or symmetric exchange verbs (e.g. buy, take, rent, trade). marks the price or item surrendered in exchange ('bought bread for five coins', 'traded a coat for a hat'). contrasts with allative (`an`), which marks the item gained when selling.
- **sustenance**: triggered by survival or feeding verbs. marks what an organism lives on ('survives on leaves').
