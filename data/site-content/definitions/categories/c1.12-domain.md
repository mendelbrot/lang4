---
id: c1.12
name: domain
---

the eight unary cv postfixes (`x-op`) that shift a lexical stem out of its default home domain and into one of the eight ontological domains (`c2.01–c2.08`).

each suffix maps one-to-one onto a lexical domain: matter (`zu` -> `c2.01`), biology (`no` -> `c2.02`), motion (`re` -> `c2.03`), artifact (`ro` -> `c2.04`), sense (`ku` -> `c2.05`), mind (`me` -> `c2.06`), society (`ga` -> `c2.07`), and form (`pe` -> `c2.08`). attaching a domain postfix projects a root's core image-schema into a new conceptual realm, acting as a systematic metaphor and derivation engine. domain postfixes are omitted when a root is used in its default domain.
