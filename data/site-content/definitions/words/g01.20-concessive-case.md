id: g01.20
short: concessive case
---

obstacle overcome or contrast ('despite, in spite of, although, however'). reason quartet 4/4.

- **obstacle**: activated with event, force, or state nouns. marks a real or hypothetical barrier that fails to block the clause ('despite the rain, in spite of fear').
- **discourse.contrast**: activated when attached to a discourse pointer (`that-before`). links clauses by contrast ('however, but, despite that, nevertheless').
