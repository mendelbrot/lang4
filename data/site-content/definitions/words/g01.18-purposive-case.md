---
id: g01.18
short: purposive case
---

intended future motive or goal ('for the goal of, in order to'). reason quartet 2/4.

- **goal**: activated with volitional action verbs with target nouns. marks a desired, unrealized outcome motivating the action.
- **discourse.purpose**: activated when attached to a discourse pointer (`that-before` or `this-after`). links clauses by intention ('for that purpose, in order to').

contrasts with causal (`ot`), which marks a realized cause rather than an unrealized future goal.
