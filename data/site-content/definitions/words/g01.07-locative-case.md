---
id: g01.07
short: locative case
---

static location in space or time ('at, in, on, during').

- **spatial**: activated with place, surface, or container nouns. marks static physical position without motion toward or away.
- **temporal**: activated with time nouns (e.g. day, night, noon, winter). marks the point or window in time when the event occurs.
- **discourse.concurrent**: activated when attached to a discourse pointer (`that-before`). links two clauses as simultaneous ('meanwhile, at that same time').
