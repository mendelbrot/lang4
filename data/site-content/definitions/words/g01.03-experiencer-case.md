---
id: g01.03
short: experiencer case
---

conscious perceiver, feeler, or mental and sensory state holder.

- **perception**: activated with sensory verbs (e.g. see, hear, smell, feel) with an accusative (`om`) stimulus. marks involuntary or receptive perception, contrasting with nominative (`es`) for deliberate observation (e.g. looking at, listening to).
- **emotion**: activated with emotional or cognitive verbs (e.g. fear, love, enjoy, remember). marks the conscious feeler or thinker.
- **sensation**: activated with intransitive state (`id`) or process (`uc`) verbs describing internal bodily or mental conditions (e.g. cold, tired, hungry). contrasts with accusative (`om`) to distinguish felt experience ('i am cold') from objective physical state ('the water is cold').
