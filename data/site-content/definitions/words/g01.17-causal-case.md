short: causal case
---

realized cause or reason ('because of, caused by, therefore'). reason quartet 1/4.

- **cause**: activated with event, force, or state nouns. marks an actual, realized fact that brought about the clause.
- **reciprocity**: activated with social or legal response verbs (e.g. thank, reward, fine, punish). marks the prior deed that prompted the response ('thanked her for her help').
- **discourse.consequence**: activated when attached to a discourse pointer (`that-before`). links clauses by cause and effect ('because of that, therefore, so').
