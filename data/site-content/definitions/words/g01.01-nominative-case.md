---
id: g01.01
short: nominative case
---

volitional agent, actor, or comparee. marks the deliberate initiator of an action or the subject being evaluated in a comparison.

- **agent**: activated with transitive (`at`) verbs. marks the volitional doer acting upon an accusative (`om`) object.
- **actor**: activated with intransitive process (`uc`) verbs. marks a volitional performer of a self-contained action (e.g. walking, running). contrasts with accusative (`om`) for non-volitional processes and experiencer (`et`) for involuntary sensations.
- **comparee**: activated with comparative (`ak`) verbs. marks the entity being measured against the accusative (`om`) standard.
