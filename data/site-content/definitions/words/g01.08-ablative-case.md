---
id: g01.08
short: ablative case
---

starting point in space, time, or state ('from, origin, source, since').

- **spatial.origin**: activated with motion or transfer verbs with place nouns. marks the point of departure or physical source.
- **temporal.start**: activated with time nouns. marks the starting boundary in time ('since, starting from').
- **state.origin**: activated with change or transformation verbs. marks the initial state or material from which something transforms.
- **discourse.sequence**: activated when attached to a discourse pointer (`that-before`). links two clauses sequentially ('after that, and then, since then').
