---
id: g01.09
short: allative case
---

endpoint in space, time, state, or exchange ('to, toward, destination, until, into').

- **spatial.destination**: activated with motion or placement verbs with place nouns. marks the physical target or arrival point.
- **spatial.direction**: activated with orientation or aimed verbs (e.g. face, shout, swing). marks direction without implying physical arrival or impact.
- **temporal.end**: activated with time nouns. marks the ending boundary in time ('until, up to').
- **state.result**: activated with change or transformation verbs (e.g. melt, freeze, turn). marks the resulting state or form ('into').
- **exchange.gain**: activated with outgoing exchange verbs (e.g. sell, give, convert) where the accusative (`om`) is the item parted with. marks the money, item, or value gained in return ('sold for five coins').
- **discourse.endpoint**: activated when attached to a discourse pointer (`that-before`). means 'until then, up to that point'.
