---
id: g01.15
short: abessive case
---

absence, lack, or exclusion ('without, lacking, except for, excluding').

- **absence**: activated with any verb and a missing participant, tool, or feature ('without a map, lacking salt').
- **exclusory**: activated in clauses containing universal or group quantifiers (e.g. all, everyone, every day). marks the member excluded from the set ('except for sam, excluding sunday').
