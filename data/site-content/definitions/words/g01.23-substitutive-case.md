id: g01.23
short: substitutive case
---

replaced entity or event ('instead of, rather than, in place of').

- **replacement**: activated with any noun parallel to another participant in the clause. marks the entity bypassed or stood in for ('drank tea instead of coffee, went in place of alex').
- **discourse.alternative**: activated when attached to a discourse pointer (`that-before`). links clauses as alternatives ('instead of that, rather than doing that').
