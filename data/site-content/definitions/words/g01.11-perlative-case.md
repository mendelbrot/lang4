---
id: g01.11
short: perlative case
---

path, route, or conduit traversed ('along, through, via').

- **spatial.path**: activated with motion verbs with path, opening, or medium nouns (e.g. road, forest, tunnel, air). marks the route or medium moved through.
- **channel**: activated with communication or transfer verbs with conduit nouns (e.g. mail, radio, messenger). marks the channel through which something is sent.
