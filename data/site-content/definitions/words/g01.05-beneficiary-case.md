---
id: g01.05
short: beneficiary case
---

marks a participant who benefits from the clause.

- **benefit**: activated with any action or state verb. marks the entity for whose advantage, well-being, or sake the event occurs ('for the benefit of').
- **proxy**: activated with volitional action verbs when an agent acts on someone else's behalf. contrasts with substitutive (`og`), which emphasizes replacing someone rather than helping them.
