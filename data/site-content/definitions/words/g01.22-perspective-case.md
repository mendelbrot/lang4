short: perspective case
---

frame of reference, viewpoint, or lens ('according to, from the viewpoint of, as for').

- **viewpoint**: activated with animate or experiencer nouns. marks the person whose subjective judgment, standard, or perception frames the clause ('to me, according to sam').
- **domain**: activated with abstract category or dimension nouns (e.g. cost, law, ethics, weather). narrows the scope or criterion being evaluated ('in terms of cost, as for weather').
